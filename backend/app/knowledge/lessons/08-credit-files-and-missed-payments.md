## What a credit file contains

A credit file is a record held by a credit reference agency. It can include information about credit accounts, how they have been managed, applications and public-record information. Lenders may use information from credit reference agencies alongside other details when assessing an application. Different lenders can make different decisions, so a credit file does not guarantee approval or rejection.

There is not necessarily one identical file shared by every organisation. Information can differ between agencies, and lenders may report to some agencies but not others. You can check your reports with the UK credit reference agencies and look for accounts, addresses or events you do not recognise. Use the agencies' official channels and protect your identity information.

## What a missed payment may mean

If a payment is late or missed, the provider may contact you, charge fees or report account information to a credit reference agency, depending on the agreement and circumstances. The details can affect how a future lender views your application, but no single entry tells the whole story and its effect can vary.

Contact the provider as soon as you think you may not be able to pay. Explain what has changed and ask what options are available. If a payment has already been missed, acting promptly can help you understand the next steps. Do not ignore notices, and do not promise an amount that you cannot realistically afford.

Sometimes information is wrong or incomplete. If you find an error, contact the organisation that supplied it and the credit reference agency, and keep copies of what you send. Explain clearly which entry you believe is inaccurate and provide relevant evidence without sharing more personal information than necessary.

## Focus on accurate information and support

Checking your file can help you spot mistakes and understand what information is visible. It does not mean you need to buy a service or pay someone to repair your credit history. Be cautious of claims that an organisation can remove accurate information or guarantee a particular score.

If you are behind on payments or struggling with several debts, a credit-file question may be only one part of the situation. Prioritise immediate essentials, speak to the providers involved and get free, independent support. MoneyHelper explains credit reports, and StepChange can provide debt guidance. Rules and processes can vary across the UK, so check current guidance for your location.
